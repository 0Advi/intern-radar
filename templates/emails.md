# Email templates

Skeletons, not mail-merge. Every email is rewritten per company; `{…}` parts must be researched and true.
Length 110–160 words. Plain text, no formatting, no emojis. Gmail drafts carry the tailored resume PDF as
an attachment; keep the "Resume: {Drive link}" line as a backup, and do not write "attached" (a draft
whose attachment fails would then promise a file that is not there).

## Subject lines (pick one, keep under 60 chars)
- `Intern application — {role/team} | MIT Bengaluru '27`
- `Backend intern, Jan 2027 — built {relevant thing}`
- `{Company} {team} internship — Aditya, MIT Bengaluru`

## A. Open posting exists

Hi {first name},

I saw the {role} opening at {Company} ({where}). {One specific, true line about why this team — product,
launch, blog post, stack}.

I'm a final-year CS student at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru (graduating July 2027).
Two things that map to this role:
- {Selling point 1, tied to their work}
- {Selling point 2, tied to their work}

I'm available for a {duration} internship from {start}, and I'm looking for a role with a path to full time.
Resume: {Drive link}
Code: github.com/0Advi

Would you be the right person to consider this, or could you point me to who is?

Thanks,
Aditya Kumar Singh
{phone}

## C. Generic HR / careers@ inbox

Subject must name the role. Body as A, opening with:
"I'm writing to apply for a {role} internship at {Company} starting {start}."

## Follow-up 1 (3 working days, reply in the same thread)

Hi {first name}, following up on my note below about a {role} internship. Since then {only if true: one
new thing — shipped feature, new commit, etc.; otherwise drop this sentence}. Happy to share anything
else that helps. Thanks — Aditya

## Follow-up 2 (5 working days later, last one)

Hi {first name}, a last note in case this got buried. If internships aren't open now, I'd be glad to
know when to check back. Thanks for your time — Aditya

## Replies to responses (always approved by Aditya before sending)
- **Interview / call invite:** thank them, confirm interest, offer 2–3 concrete slots Aditya has given,
  ask about format. Don't raise pay first.
- **"Send details / fill this form":** answer exactly what's asked; forms are filled by Aditya.
- **Stipend asked:** state the range Aditya has approved for that company; never improvise numbers.
- **Rejection / not hiring:** two-line thank-you, ask to be kept in mind; mark `closed`.
- **Asks to stop:** apologise in one line, mark `do_not_contact`.
